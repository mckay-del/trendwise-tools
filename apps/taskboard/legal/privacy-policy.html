McKay's Taskboard: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

McKay's Taskboard ("the app") is a private productivity tool operated by K. McKay Larsen EA, Ltd., doing business as Trendwise Business Services ("we", "us"), Las Vegas, Nevada. It is used only by the owner to organize their own work across their own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public or to clients.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects a Google account, the app asks Google for permission to:

  • Read email (Gmail, read-only): to find messages that call for action and turn them into tasks.
  • Read calendars (Google Calendar, read-only): to find meetings that need a response or preparation.
  • Manage tasks (Google Tasks): to show existing tasks and to save tasks created in the app, so they appear on the owner's phone.

The app cannot send, delete or change email, and cannot create, change or delete calendar events.

What the app stores, and where

  • Where: everything is stored in a database on a computer or private server controlled by the owner, not on a hosted service. This includes:
    • task titles, notes and due dates
    • links back to the related email or event
    • short excerpts of the related messages
  • Sign-ins: Google sign-in tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Reach: the app is reachable only from the owner's own devices.

Who the data is shared with

  • Anthropic (AI processing): to decide which emails and events need action, short excerpts of them are sent to Anthropic's API. This covers the sender, subject, date and part of the message text. Anthropic processes this to return task suggestions, under its commercial terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal). Under those terms, API data is not used to train its models.
  • No one else: we do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google API Services: Limited Use

McKay's Taskboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the app's task features to the owner. It is never transferred for any other purpose, and no person reads it except the owner.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Disconnecting: an account can be disconnected at any time in the app's Settings. That revokes the app's access and deletes its stored sign-in.
  • Removing access in Google: access can also be removed at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting everything: delete the app's data folder.
